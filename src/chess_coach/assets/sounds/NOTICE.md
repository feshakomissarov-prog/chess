# Chess Coach sound assets

These three bundled WAV files are converted to mono 16-bit PCM at 44.1 kHz from
the following Freesound CC0 recordings by `el_boss`. The original pages offer
the files under CC0 1.0; the audio itself has not been artistically approved by
the Chess Coach owner yet.

for private comparison. They are not included in the project. Lichess's COPYING
notice marks its other `public/sound` files non-free; therefore none were copied
or redistributed. The MyInstants page does not provide a redistribution license.
